# Prompts para ChatGPT · Personajes de Pay the Piper

Personajes sacados del borrador del argumento. **Protagonista decidido (2026-11-21): el Flautista adulto, que vuelve a Venecia a reclamar su deuda** (su hoja ya está hecha). Tano y la Nonna Bettina pasan a ser aliados (Tano es el niño cojo de Hamelin, que no envejece por la deuda). Adjunta siempre la hoja del Flautista o la de Il Dottore para que copie el estilo. Pega primero el bloque de estilo y luego el del personaje. Público: alumnos de 14 años en adelante, tono oscuro (cambiado el 2026-11-21).

## Bloque de estilo (siempre)

```
Character model sheet in the exact same style as the attached image: dark hand-drawn ink and watercolor, gritty gothic engraving texture, pure black background, palette of black, bone white, crimson and sickly green. Venice during the 1630 plague, historically inspired costumes. Dark, grim and mature tone for a teenage audience (14+): menace, decay, grime and visible signs of the plague are welcome, but no extreme gore. Layout: front, side and back full-body views in a row, a close-up portrait on the top right, separate drawings of key items, and one dynamic action pose. No text, no labels.
```

## Protagonista y aliados

### Tano (aliado: el niño cojo que se quedó en Hamelin)

```
Character: Tano, a boy who looks 12 years old but has not aged for centuries: he is the lame child who stayed behind in Hamelin in 1284, bound by the unpaid debt. Now a Venetian street boy who plays a cheap wooden whistle for coins on the bridges. He walks with a simple wooden crutch, shown with dignity. Thin, messy dark hair, old and tired eyes in a child's face. Patched medieval-looking shirt under a threadbare Venetian jacket, cloth-wrapped feet, a faded German pilgrim badge from Hamelin pinned to his chest. Key items: the crutch, the whistle, the old badge. Action pose: limping fast across a bridge in the fog, looking back over his shoulder.
```

### Pulce (rata compañera)

```
Character: Pulce, a small clever scout rat, the companion of the Piper (the hooded man in crimson from the attached image). Grey-brown fur, one ear bitten with a notch, bright curious dark eyes (not glowing red, she is friendly), long pink tail. Expressive but still a realistic rat, not a cartoon. Show her from front, side and back, a close-up of her face, and several poses: sitting up sniffing, running, peeking out of a pouch, pointing her nose at something hidden.
```

### Ester (aliada, aprendiz de médico del Ghetto)

```
Character: Ester, a 14-year-old girl, apprentice of a Jewish doctor in the Venetian Ghetto in 1630. Intelligent, serious face, dark curly hair tied back under a simple headscarf, modest dark dress with a work apron full of small pockets, rolled sleeves. She carries her teacher's worn leather notebook full of notes, a small satchel with glass vials and herbs, and a candle lantern. Action pose: holding up the open notebook and pointing at a page, determined.
```

### Nonna Bettina (aliada, cuida de Tano)

```
Character: Nonna Bettina, an elderly woman who took in Tano (not his real grandmother), a poor Venetian woman. Kind, wrinkled face, white hair under a dark shawl, simple patched dress, wooden rosary, small hands. She is sick but gentle: show her also sitting in bed wrapped in a blanket, singing a lullaby with a candle beside her. Key item: an old wooden box of keepsakes. Warm and tender, the only soft character in the cast.
```

## Villanos y enemigos

### Alvise Morosin (villano principal)

```
Character: Alvise Morosin, Venetian "Provveditore alla Sanità" (health magistrate) in 1630, the main villain who hid the plague and broke his promise to pay the Piper. Middle-aged, well-fed, pale face with a thin smile and cold eyes, neatly trimmed beard. Rich black and deep crimson velvet senatorial robes with a fur stole, gold chain of office, rings, a sealed document with a red wax seal and a heavy coin purse he never opens. No mask: his face is his mask. Action pose: on wooden scaffolding, pointing an accusing finger while a long dark shadow behind him takes a twisted shape.
```

### Confraternita del Becco (médicos del pico rasos)

```
Character: a common member of the "Confraternita del Becco", the rank-and-file plague doctors, lower rank than "Il Dottore" in the attached image. Simpler and shabbier: plain stained beak mask with cracked dark lenses, small black hat, patched dark waxed coat, rope belt, a short wooden stick, a small bag of herbs and a net to catch rats. Hunched, nervous posture: he is scared and only following orders. Action pose: lunging forward with the net.
```

## Jefes

### La Monaca delle Ossa (capítulo 3, criptas)

```
Character: "La Monaca delle Ossa" (the Nun of Bones), guardian of the crypts under Venice. Tall, pale, in a long black and grey nun's habit with a white wimple, a rosary made of small bones, a heavy psalm book and a tall iron candelabrum. Her mouth is open singing, with pale sound waves around her. Eerie and solemn, not monstrous. Action pose: singing with arms raised in a crypt full of candles and skulls set into the walls.
```

### Il Re dei Ratti (capítulo 4, bajo San Marcos)

```
Character: "Il Re dei Ratti", a giant rat king: dozens of rats whose tails are tangled together in a huge knot, forming one big creature. Not evil but frightened and trapped: wide scared red eyes, ears flat. Dripping canal water and old coins and bones stuck in the tangle like a crown. Show the full creature from several sides, a close-up of the knotted tails, and an action pose rising out of a dark canal in panic.
```

### Il Proto dell'Arsenale (capítulo 5, el Arsenal)

```
Character: "Il Proto dell'Arsenale", master shipbuilder of the Venetian Arsenal, inside a huge armor built from ship timber, iron bands and heavy ship chains. Bearded face visible through a wooden helmet slit, glowing forge light from inside the armor. He carries a giant shipwright's mallet and drags a cage for rats. Key items: the mallet, a rat cage, a coil of chain. Action pose: smashing the mallet down, sparks and wood chips flying.
```

### La Tarantella (capítulo 6, carnaval)

```
Character: "La Tarantella", a lady of the Venetian carnival whose cursed mask forces everyone to dance without stopping. Elegant crimson and gold carnival gown with a wide skirt, a white porcelain full-face mask with a frozen smile, feathers, and a tambourine with ribbons. Puppet-like spinning movements. Behind her, silhouettes of masked dancers who cannot stop. Action pose: whirling and striking the tambourine, ribbons flying.
```

### Il Priore del Lazzaretto (capítulo 7)

```
Character: "Il Priore del Lazzaretto", the chief plague doctor of the hospital island, more imposing than "Il Dottore" in the attached image. Very tall, with the largest beak mask, dark brass-rimmed lenses glowing green, tall black hat, long heavy black coat with a dark green cape and a brass chain of office. He carries a tall staff hung with small bells and spreads thick green fog. Action pose: ringing the bell staff while green fog rolls around him.
```
